# SplineCraft Flow

<!-- last-synced:start -->
<!-- last-synced:end -->

**SplineCraft Flow 7.0.0**, for **Unreal Engine 5.8**. Rapid construction of static meshes and
actors along a spline — roads, rails, pipes, fences, curbs, bridges, tracks, rows of columns,
lamps, or trees, with a dual-mode (Line/Curve) placement system and a modifier-stack spline
geometry editor. Everything is configured on a single `ASplineCraftFlowActor` in the **Details**
panel, and the geometry rebuilds automatically on every change.

## Note on the name

SplineCraft Flow is a separate plugin from [SplineCraft](../spline-craft/index.md) — they share a
naming family but not a codebase, and installing one has no effect on the other.
